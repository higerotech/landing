Cookie notice

Effective from: 16 September 2026 Version: 1.0 Scope: higerotech.com

This site uses no cookies. None: no first-party, no third-party, no technical, no analytics, no advertising cookies. That is why you will not see a consent banner — there would be nothing to consent to.

This page exists to explain what stands in their place, and to let you verify it yourself in thirty seconds.

1. Why there are no cookies

It is not a technical feat: it follows from how the site is built. There are no sessions to maintain because there are no accounts; no analytics to feed because we decided not to measure traffic; and no third-party resources that could set their own, because everything — fonts, images, code — is served from our own domain.

The decision has a real cost and we accept it: we do not know how many people visit this site, where they come from, or which section they read most. We give up that data in exchange for not collecting it.

2. What stands in their place

One single thing: if you choose ES or EN in the switch in the top bar, we store that preference in your browser's local storage (localStorage), under the key lang, with the value es or en.

The differences from a cookie, which are the ones that matter:

A cookieThis preference
Does it travel to the server? Yes, on every request, automatically No, never. It stays on your device
Can Higerotech see it? Yes No. Our server has no way to read it
Can it identify you? Usually yes No. It is one of two possible words, the same for everyone who picks that language
How do you delete it? By clearing the site's cookies By clearing the site's data in your browser. You can also block local storage altogether: the site will keep working and will simply start in Spanish again

That is why it requires no consent: prior-consent rules exist for storage that allows a person to be tracked or identified, and this neither leaves the device nor tells anyone apart.

3. How to verify it yourself

You do not have to believe us. In any desktop browser:

  1. Open the developer tools (F12, or Ctrl+Shift+I).
  2. Go to the Application tab and look at Cookies: the list is empty.
  3. In the same tab, open Local storage and you will see the only key we store, lang, if you have already picked a language.
  4. In the Network tab, reload the page: every request goes to higerotech.com. None goes out to a third party.

That last point is the hardest to fake, and the one that protects your privacy most: if no third-party resource loads, no third party receives your IP address when you visit us.

4. The one exception: whoever delivers the page

For completeness: the bytes of this page are delivered by Cloudflare, which, like any server, sees the IP address it responds to. It sets no tracking cookies on this site, and we have not enabled retention of its logs. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy, §4.

5. What would happen if this changed

If we ever add analytics, a form, or any technology requiring consent, we will do three things before switching it on: publish here what it is and what it is for, ask for your express and revocable consent, and record the change in the repository's public history.

As long as this page still says what it says, it has not happened.
