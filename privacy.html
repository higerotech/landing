Privacy Policy

Effective from: 16 September 2026 Version: 1.0 Scope: higerotech.com

The honest summary, with the detail below. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, has no forms and no database, and builds no profile of anyone who visits it. That is not an unfinished task: it is an architectural decision, and you can verify it yourself in the site's source code.

What does happen is small, and worth stating precisely: our infrastructure provider sees your IP address for the moment it takes to deliver the page, and if you write to us by email or WhatsApp we keep your message. Nothing else.

2. What this policy covers, and what it does not

This policy covers the higerotech.com website: what happens when you visit it and when you contact us through the channels we publish.

It does not cover the applications we build or operate for our clients. That distinction is not a technicality — it determines who answers to you:

  • On this site, Higerotech decides what data is processed and why. We are the controller, and this policy is our commitment.
  • In a system we build or operate for a client, the client decides. Higerotech acts as a processor, on documented instructions and under a data processing agreement. If your data is there, that company is the controller, and its privacy policy applies to you, not this one.

If you are not sure which of the two applies to you, write to us and we will tell you.

3. What data is processed, and what is not

What does not exist on this site

  • No cookies. None: neither first-party nor third-party. That is why you will not see a consent banner — there would be nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics. We do not use Google Analytics or any equivalent tool. We do not know how many people visit this site or where they come from.
  • No forms, no accounts. Nothing to fill in, nothing to register, no password.
  • No third-party resources. Fonts, images and code are all served from our own domain. No third party receives a request from your browser when this page loads, which means none of them can see your IP address that way.
  • No profiling, no advertising. We do not track you, segment you, or sell anything to anyone.

4. Cloudflare, our infrastructure provider

This site is served through Cloudflare, Inc., which acts as a processor on our behalf. We say so explicitly because it is the part most sites leave out: however static and self-contained a site may be, someone has to deliver the bytes, and whoever delivers them sees the address they are going to.

  • Cloudflare receives your IP address and the technical details of the connection in order to deliver the page and protect it from abuse.
  • The processing is governed by Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum, which obliges it to process that data only on our behalf and on our instructions.
  • We have not enabled log retention in Cloudflare. That is a decision, not an oversight: we could turn it on for better diagnostics and have chosen not to.
  • Cloudflare publishes its own sub-processor list and privacy policy at cloudflare.com.

It is the only processor involved in delivering this site. When we add another, it will appear in this section, with its purpose, before it starts operating.

5. If you write to us

We publish an email address and a WhatsApp number instead of a contact form. That reduces the site's technical surface, but it does not remove the processing: when you write, we receive and keep your name, your email address or number, your company if you mention it, and the content of your message.

We use it only to reply and to carry forward the commercial conversation you started. We do not add it to any mailing list, we do not send newsletters you did not ask for, and we do not pass it to third parties.

One thing that is not up to us: email and WhatsApp are third-party services, and their providers process those messages under their own terms. If you need to send us something sensitive, tell us and we will agree on a suitable channel before you send it.

8. International transfers

Cloudflare runs a global network, so delivering this page may involve processing your IP address outside Venezuelan territory. We could not promise you otherwise, and you should be sceptical of any site served by a global network that tells you your data never leaves the country.

Where those transfers affect people in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, they are covered by the mechanisms incorporated into our agreement with Cloudflare: the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

The Venezuelan Constitution grants you, in article 28, the right to access the information held about you in registries of any kind, to know its use and purpose, and to request its updating, rectification or destruction where it is inaccurate or unlawfully affects your rights. Article 60 protects your private life and honour. That is what is known as habeas data, and it is the basis of this section.

In practice, you can ask us for:

  • Access: what we hold about you and what we use it for.
  • Rectification: correcting anything that is wrong.
  • Erasure: deleting your correspondence before the 24-month period.
  • Objection: that we stop processing your data for commercial purposes.
  • A copy of what you sent us, in a readable format.

How: write to contacto@higerotech.com from the address you are asking about, or give us something that lets us locate the conversation. We answer within 30 calendar days. If we cannot meet a request, we will tell you why and what the alternative is.

We do not charge for handling these requests. If a request were manifestly excessive or repetitive, we would tell you before doing anything, not afterwards.

If you live in a jurisdiction whose law grants you equivalent rights — the European Union, the United Kingdom, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico, Chile, Argentina or California, among others — you can exercise them through the same channel and the same procedure. We do not keep separate processes depending on where the person asking lives.

10. Security

What we do, without embellishment:

  • All traffic is encrypted with TLS.
  • The server does not write to its own file system and runs without unnecessary privileges.
  • The site applies strict security headers and a content policy that prevents loading code from another origin.
  • The site has no database, no sessions and no admin panel. A good part of our security consists of there being nothing to steal.
  • Security checks run automatically on every change to the code.

What we will not tell you: we are not certified under ISO 27001, SOC 2 or PCI DSS, and we will not claim to be until we are. Nor do we promise that no incident is possible. If you find a vulnerability, our disclosure policy is in the SECURITY.md file of the site's public repository.

11. Minors

This site is aimed at companies and professionals, not at minors, and it does not knowingly collect data from children or adolescents. If you believe we have received a minor's data through our contact channels, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Applicable legal framework

Precision matters here, because this is a field rich in comfortable claims:

  • Venezuela currently has no general data protection statute, and no national supervisory authority for the matter. What protects your data are articles 28 and 60 of the Constitution and the case law of the Constitutional Chamber of the Supreme Court that develops them, which binds private parties too.
  • We do not claim "GDPR compliance", because that claim carries a whole apparatus of formal obligations that a company of this size does not have in place. What we do is apply its principles — minimisation, purpose limitation, defined retention, transparency — and describe in this document exactly what happens, which is the substance of the regulation.
  • This policy is governed by Venezuelan law. Its interpretation and any disputes are subject to our Terms of Use.
